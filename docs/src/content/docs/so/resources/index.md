## Qaybaha Kheyraadka

### 🔧 Kheyraadka Farsamada

Qalabka iyo goobaha muhiimka ah ee isku dubaridka ammaan ah:

- **[Signal](https://signal.org)** — Farriinta sirto ah (qalabka isgaarsiinta aasaasiga ah)
- **[CryptPad](https://cryptpad.fr)** — Dukumeentiyada iskaashiga ee sirto ah
- **[Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org)** — Baaritaanka internetka oo aan magac lahayn
- **Hagayaasha amniga qalabka** — Ilaalinta taleefannada iyo kumbuyuutarrada

→ **[Arag dhammaan qalabka farsamada](/so/resources/technical/)**

### 📄 Agabka La Daabici Karo

Kheyraadka diyaar u ah daabacaadda bulshadaada:

- **Kaardka xuquuqda** English, Español, Soomaali, iyo العربية
- **Liisaska hubinta soo dhaweynta** xubnaha cusub
- **Qaababka hubinta amniga** dib u eegista bishii
- **Kaardka doorka qaybta** sharxaya mas'uuliyadaha
- **Foomamka xiriirka degdegga ah**

→ **[Arag dhammaan agabka la daabici karo](/so/resources/printable/)**

### 📚 Hagayaasha Hirgelinta

Tilmaamaha tallaabo-tallaabo ee bilowga iyo maaraynta unug:

- **[Hagaha Kooxda Aasaasiga](/so/start-a-cell/founding-team/)** — Sida loo bilaabo 3-5 qof oo la isku halayn karo
- **[Dejinta Signal](/so/start-a-cell/signal-setup/)** — Dejinta farriinta ammaan ah
- **[Habka Soo Dhaweynta](/so/start-a-cell/onboarding/)** — Hubinta iyo tababarka xubnaha cusub
- **[Dhaqannada Amniga](/so/security/)** — Aasaaska amniga hawlgalka

### 🌍 Kheyraadka Luqado-Badan

Dukumeentiga waxaa lagu heli karaa luqado badan:

- **English** (dhamaystiran)
- **Español / Spanish** (89% dhamaystiran)
- **Soomaali / Somali** (89% dhamaystiran)
- **العربية / Arabic** (89% dhamaystiran)

Hel nuxurka la turjumay iyada oo loo marayo xulashada luqadda ee menu-ga socodka.

## Isticmaalka Kheyraadkan

### Dadka Shakhsi ahaaneed

Haddii aad wax ka baranayso qaabka ama aad ka fikirayso bilowga unug:

1. Ku billow **[Qaabka](/so/model/)** si aad u fahamto qaab-dhismeedka
2. Dib u eeg **[Dhaqannada Amniga](/so/security/)** si aad u fahamto amniga hawlgalka
3. Sahami **[Qalabka Farsamada](/so/resources/technical/)** si aad u aragto goobaha aad isticmaali doonto
4. Soo deji **[Kaardka Xuquuqda](/so/rights/print-cards/)** si aad ula socoto

### Kooxaha Aasaasiga

Haddii aad bilowday unug 3-5 qof oo la isku halayn karo:

1. Dhamaystir **[Hagaha Kooxda Aasaasiga](/so/start-a-cell/founding-team/)**
2. Deji **[Signal](/so/start-a-cell/signal-setup/)** dhammaan aasaasayaasha
3. Isticmaal **[Liisaska Hubinta La Daabici Karo](/so/resources/printable/)** si aad u raacdo horumarinta
4. Dib u eeg **[Habka Soo Dhaweynta](/so/start-a-cell/onboarding/)** ka hor inta aadan ku darin xubnaha

### Unugyada Firfircoon

Haddii aad maamulay unug la dhisay:

1. Samee **[Hubinta Amniga](/so/resources/printable/#liiska-hubinta-hubinta-amniga)** bishii
2. Isticmaal **[Qaababka](/so/resources/printable/)** habab joogto ah
3. Qaybso **[Kaardka Xuquuqda](/so/rights/print-cards/)** xubnaha bulshada
4. Dib u eeg **[Dhaqannada Amniga](/so/security/)** si joogto ah dhammaan xubnaha

## Ku Biirinta Kheyraadka

Ma haysaa qaab, hage, ama qalab ka caawin kara unugyo kale? Mashruucan wuxuu soo dhaweynayaa wax ku biirinta.

Eeg [kaydka GitHub](https://github.com/mkokes/soteria-model) tilmaamaha wax ku biirinta.
